## Key ideas

- Run a fixed checklist on any sequence: differences, second differences, ratios, alternating/interleaved subsequences, squares/cubes/primes/factorials, and "each term from the previous two".
- Letters: convert to positions (A = 1 … Z = 26) and reuse the number checklist; wrap around after Z if the pattern needs it.
- **Recurrences** like $u_{n+1}=2u_n+1$ are easiest to handle by computing the first few terms and spotting the closed form (here $u_n+1$ doubles).
- **Rewriting rules** (e.g. "replace A by AB, B by A"): apply them to every symbol *simultaneously*, step by step, and track lengths — they often follow Fibonacci-like counts.
- For **figure/grid patterns**, count something (tiles, edges, dots) in the first 3–4 figures and turn it into a number sequence.

## Common traps

- Committing to the first pattern that fits three terms; check it against *all* given terms.
- Applying rewrite rules one symbol at a time in place, so new symbols are rewritten again in the same step.
- Off-by-one errors between "the 10th term" and "after 10 steps".
