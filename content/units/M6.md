## Key ideas

- **Exponents**: $a^ma^n=a^{m+n}$, $(a^m)^n=a^{mn}$, $a^{-n}=1/a^n$, $a^{1/n}=\sqrt[n]{a}$.
- **Logarithms**: $\log_a x=y\iff a^y=x$; $\log(xy)=\log x+\log y$, $\log(x^k)=k\log x$, change of base $\log_a x=\frac{\ln x}{\ln a}$. Only positive arguments.
- Solve exponential equations by writing both sides with a common base, or by taking logs.
- **Exact values** at $0,\frac\pi6,\frac\pi4,\frac\pi3,\frac\pi2$ and their multiples; use the unit circle for signs in each quadrant.
- Periodicity and symmetry: $\sin(\pi-x)=\sin x$, $\cos(-x)=\cos x$, period $2\pi$ for sin/cos, $\pi$ for tan.
- All solutions of $\sin x=c$: $x=\alpha+2k\pi$ or $x=\pi-\alpha+2k\pi$. Of $\cos x=c$: $x=\pm\alpha+2k\pi$.
- Identities on the formula sheet: Pythagorean, sum/difference and double angle.
- **Sinusoid** $f(t)=d+a\sin(b(t-c))$: amplitude $|a|$, period $\frac{2\pi}{b}$, phase shift $c$, midline $y=d$.

## Common traps

- Losing the second family of solutions of a trig equation.
- Working in degrees when the question is in radians.
- Writing $\log(x+y)=\log x+\log y$ (false).
