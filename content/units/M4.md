## Key ideas

- $f'>0$: increasing; $f'<0$: decreasing. **Critical points** where $f'=0$ (or undefined).
- First-derivative test: a sign change $+\to-$ gives a local maximum, $-\to+$ a local minimum. Second-derivative test: $f''(c)<0$ max, $f''(c)>0$ min.
- **Inflection points**: $f''$ changes sign.
- **Global extremes on $[a,b]$**: compare $f$ at the critical points *and* at the endpoints.
- **Optimisation**: write the quantity as a function of one variable using the constraint, then find the extremum on the allowed domain.
- **Related rates**: write an equation linking the quantities, differentiate with respect to time, then substitute the known values.
- Position $s(t)$, velocity $v=s'$, acceleration $a=v'$; speed is $|v|$.

## Common traps

- Forgetting the endpoints when the domain is closed.
- Substituting numbers *before* differentiating in related-rates problems.
- Calling every point with $f''=0$ an inflection point (the sign must change).
