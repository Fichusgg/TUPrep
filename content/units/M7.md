## Key ideas

- Areas: triangle $\frac12bh$ or $\frac12ab\sin C$; circle $\pi r^2$; sector $\frac12r^2\theta$. Volumes: cylinder $\pi r^2h$, cone and pyramid $\frac13\cdot\text{base}\cdot h$, sphere $\frac43\pi r^3$.
- **Sine rule** $\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B}$; **cosine rule** $c^2=a^2+b^2-2ab\cos C$.
- Lines: slopes of perpendicular lines multiply to $-1$. Circle: $(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2$.
- Intersections: substitute the line into the circle and solve the quadratic (the discriminant gives 0, 1 or 2 points).
- **Vectors**: length $\|v\|=\sqrt{v_1^2+v_2^2}$; dot product $u\cdot v=u_1v_1+u_2v_2=\|u\|\|v\|\cos\theta$; orthogonal iff $u\cdot v=0$.
- Motion: position $\mathbf r(t)$, velocity $\mathbf r'(t)$, speed $\|\mathbf r'(t)\|$, acceleration $\mathbf r''(t)$.

## Common traps

- Using the sine rule in the ambiguous case without checking both angles.
- Forgetting to take the square root for a vector's length.
- Mixing up the angle opposite a side in the cosine rule.
