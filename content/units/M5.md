## Key ideas

- Antiderivatives from the formula sheet: $\int x^a\,dx=\frac{x^{a+1}}{a+1}+C\ (a\ne-1)$, $\int\frac1x\,dx=\ln|x|+C$, $\int e^x\,dx=e^x+C$, $\int\sin x\,dx=-\cos x+C$, $\int\cos x\,dx=\sin x+C$.
- **Linear inner function**: $\int f(ax+b)\,dx=\frac1a F(ax+b)+C$.
- **FTC**: $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx=F(b)-F(a)$, and $\frac{d}{dx}\int_a^x f(t)\,dt=f(x)$.
- **u-substitution**: spot an inner function whose derivative also appears; change the limits along with the variable.
- **Area** between curves: $\int_a^b (\text{top}-\text{bottom})\,dx$. **Mean value** of $f$ on $[a,b]$: $\frac1{b-a}\int_a^b f$.
- **Volume of revolution** about the $x$-axis: $V=\pi\int_a^b f(x)^2\,dx$.

## Common traps

- Forgetting the factor $\frac1a$ for $f(ax+b)$.
- Not changing the limits in a substitution (or changing them and then substituting back as well).
- Treating area below the axis as positive in a definite integral (it counts negatively).
- Forgetting $\pi$ or the square in volumes.
